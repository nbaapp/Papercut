## 5. Components & data

### `Fold` (struct) and `FoldAnchor` (enum)

`Fold { FoldAnchor Anchor; float Depth; }`. Depth is the distance from the anchoring edge to the crease (edge folds) or from the corner along each adjacent edge to where the crease meets them (corner folds), in sheet-local units. `Depth > 0` for a real fold. `Fold.IsCorner`, `Fold.IsEdge` helpers.

### `Crease` (struct)

`Point` (a point on the line), `Direction` (unit, along the line), `FlapNormal` (unit, perpendicular, pointing into the flap side). Sheet-local space. Signed distance `s(p) = dot(p − Point, FlapNormal)`; `s > 0` is the flap side.

### `ConvexPolygon`

Immutable list of vertices (CCW). `FromRect(Rect)`; `ClipToHalfPlane(point, normal, keepPositive)` (single-plane Sutherland–Hodgman); `Reflect(Crease)`; `Area`; `Bounds`; `IsEmpty`. Everything folding touches is convex: the sheet rect, the flap (rect ∩ half-plane), its reflection, and box footprints.

### `FoldGeometry` (static)

- `CreaseOf(Fold)`: edge N at `y = H/2 − d` with normal +y, etc.; corner NE: line through `(W/2 − d, H/2)` and `(W/2, H/2 − d)`, normal toward the corner (unit `(1,1)/√2`).
- `FlapRegion(Fold)` = sheet rect clipped to the flap side. `LandedRegion(Fold)` = `FlapRegion.Reflect(crease)` — where the flap lies after folding; this is what is drawn and what can overhang.
- `HiddenRect(Fold)`: **flap ∪ landed, within the sheet, is always an axis-aligned rectangle** — for an edge fold the strip of depth `2d` from the anchoring edge; for a corner fold the square of side `d` at the corner (reflecting the two sheet edges across the 45° line gives the square's other two sides; for `d > H` the parts of the square that would reflect outside the sheet are exactly the parts already outside the sheet, so `square ∩ sheet` stays exact — covered by a test). Every coverage and validity test is therefore rect-vs-rect. (Review B3.)
- `BackPose(Fold)` → `(Vector2 position, float rotationDegrees)` for the Back root, sheet-local. Derivation: a Back point `b` appears at `Reflect(BackToFront(b))`. `BackToFront` is a mirror; reflection across the crease is a mirror; two mirrors compose to a **rotation** by `2θ + 180°` (θ = crease angle) about the point where the Back origin lands: `position = Reflect((0,0))`. So the Back root is posed with a plain rotation + translation and every Back child (sprites, colliders, cameras) lands correctly with no scale flips. Checked by test with concrete numbers (horizontal crease at `y = c`: `(x, y) → (−x, 2c − y)`; vertical crease at `x = c`: `(x, y) → (2c + x, y)`).
- `MaxDepth(anchor)`: edge = the sheet extent perpendicular to that edge (11 or 8.5); corner = `W + H` (beyond which the crease no longer intersects the sheet). Larger depths are clamped by the input; validity already rejects any fold that leaves the player nowhere to stand.
- `DepthForDragPoint(anchor, local)`: **the grabbed edge/corner follows the cursor.** Edge: the edge lands at distance `2d` inward, so `d = inwardDistance / 2`. Corner: the corner lands at `(d, d)` from the corner along both edges, so `d = (u + v) / 2` with `u, v` the cursor's inward distances along the two edges. Clamped to `[0, MaxDepth]`.
- `DistanceToCrease(Fold, local)`: distance from a point to the crease **segment** (the part of the line inside the sheet rect) — used for the click-to-unfold hit test.
- `Coverage(Rect footprint, Fold, SheetFace face)` — footprint in **face-local space** (Front-space for Front objects, Back-space for Back objects; review B5). Front: hidden = `HiddenRect`; `Covered` if the footprint is inside it, `Uncovered` if disjoint, else `Partial` with the visible remainder = `footprint − HiddenRect`, which is **up to four axis-aligned rects** (exact; review B3). Back: the exposed region in Back-space is `BackToFront(flap half-plane) ∩ sheet` — one half-plane cut, so the visible remainder of a `Partial` Back footprint is a single convex polygon (review B4). Result `Covered` = whole footprint exposed, `Uncovered` = none.

### `FoldCoverage` / `CoverageResult`

`enum FoldCoverage { Uncovered, Partial, Covered }`. For Front objects "Covered" means hidden; for Back objects it means exposed. `CoverageResult` carries the enum plus `VisibleParts` (`IReadOnlyList<ConvexPolygon>`, **face-local**): the parts of the footprint that still exist in the world — the whole footprint when a Front object is `Uncovered` or a Back object is `Covered` (exposed); nothing when a Front object is `Covered` or a Back object is `Uncovered`; the clipped remainder(s) for `Partial`.

### `IFoldOccludee` (interface)

```csharp
public interface IFoldOccludee
{
    /// Footprint in face-local space (Front-space under Front, Back-space under Back), computed by
    /// FoldFootprint.FaceLocalRect from the collider's offset/size through faceRoot.worldToLocal * localToWorld
    /// of its four corners - exact under any pose of the Back root. SheetOcclusion does every other conversion.
    Rect FaceLocalFootprint(Transform faceRoot);
    /// Called by SheetOcclusion whenever committed folds change (including reset to none).
    void OnFoldCoverageChanged(in CoverageResult coverage, Sheet sheet);
}
```

Occlusion never touches an occludee's GameObject or colliders itself; each occludee decides — Front and Back alike (review B1). A shared helper `OccludedBoxCollider` (`Scripts/Fold/OccludedBoxCollider.cs`) applies a `CoverageResult` to one `BoxCollider2D`: all visible → box on; nothing visible → box off; partial → box off and a lazily added `PolygonCollider2D` (same `isTrigger` as the box) with one path per visible part, converted from face-local to the object's local space. `TerrainRegion` uses it (review N1 is moot after revision 2: exits are not objects). Objects under Front/Back without this interface are affected by rendering only (the composite hides them); if they carry a `Collider2D`, `SheetOcclusion` logs an error once at Awake (a collider that folding cannot see is an authoring mistake, not a silent gap).

### `SheetOcclusion` (component, on the Sheet prefab)

- Responsibility: apply committed folds to the sheet's content.
- `Apply(IReadOnlyList<Fold>)`:
  0. **Only the Screen has live physics** (round-2 B1, revision 3). A walkable overhang lies on top of a neighbour, whose walls and terrain would otherwise stop the player. So a sheet's occludee colliders, walls and edge strips exist only while it is the Screen: `Sheet` tracks `IsScreen` (set in `NotifyPlayerEntered`/`NotifyPlayerLeft`); `SheetOcclusion.Apply` and `SheetBoundary.Rebuild` run on both events as well as on `Changed`, and when `IsScreen` is false every occludee gets an empty `CoverageResult` (nothing visible → colliders off) and the outline is empty (no walls, no edges). The player is only ever on the Screen, so nothing observable is lost; the room test below therefore cannot use physics queries and is a pure rect test instead.
  1. **The Back root is always active** (review B1); `Sheet.Awake` no longer deactivates it. Back content exists physically only through its occludees' responses: with no fold every Back occludee is told `Uncovered` (nothing visible → colliders off) and every Front occludee `Uncovered` (everything visible → colliders on). Back root pose = identity.
  2. With the (single) fold: Back root pose = `BackPose(fold)`; every Front and Back occludee is notified with its `Coverage` and responds itself — no `SetActive` anywhere in occlusion.
  3. **Lifted region is unreachable** (review B2): the crease is a wall in `WalkableOutline`, rebuilt by `SheetBoundary` on the same `Changed` event (revision 2 replaced the earlier lifted-region collider). The player can never be on the lifted side at commit because validity rejects that.
  4. Occludees are gathered each `Apply` via `GetComponentsInChildren<IFoldOccludee>(includeInactive: true)` on Front and Back — sheets have tens of objects, `Apply` runs on commit/unfold only. The initial `Apply(empty)` runs in **`Start`**, not `Awake`, so every occludee has cached its collider (review N2); handlers also fetch lazily.
  5. Preview folds never reach `Apply` (rendering only). The Back camera renders the whole Back-space every frame it is enabled; only the flap region is sampled by the Flap mesh, so a preview already shows exactly the Back walls/exits the player will get.
- Multi-fold note (single-fold piece, isolated): step 2 poses the whole Back root by one fold. With several folds each flap needs its own Back copy; that is contained in `Apply` and documented there. The model, coverage tests, meshes and textures are per-fold already.
- Awake validation: `Collider2D` under Front/Back on an object with no `IFoldOccludee` in its parents → `LogError` naming it. (Physics2D layer matrix: the new layers collide with everything by default, so `SheetFront`/`SheetBack` colliders still collide with the player — required; review N8.)

### `RenderTextureFoldRenderer` (component, on the Sheet prefab, implements `IFoldRenderer`)

- Responsibility: draw one sheet as *Base mesh textured from the Front render texture* + *landed-Flap mesh textured from the Back render texture, UV-mapped through the reflection* + crease lines. The lifted region (the flap's original place) is simply not drawn, so the Desk shows through.
- Creates at `OnEnable`, destroys at `OnDisable`:
  - `FrontCamera` child of `Front` root, `BackCamera` child of `Back` root (so it rides the Back pose and always renders Back-space): orthographic, size `H/2`, local position `(0, 0, −5)`, near 0.1 / far 10, `cullingMask` = its face layer only, clear to `(0,0,0,0)`, `depth = −100` (before the main camera), `targetTexture` = its RT, URP additional data left at defaults (Base camera, no post-processing).
  - Two `RenderTexture`s of `round(W·ppu) × round(H·ppu)`, ARGB32.
  - Three child MeshRenderer objects on the Sheet root (layer Default): `Base` (z +0.05), `Flap` (z +0.02), `Creases` (z +0.01). The player is at z −0.5 in `Desk.unity` and the camera looks along +z, so the player is always drawn above everything here; neighbouring sheets' Base meshes are at +0.05, so an overhanging Flap at +0.02 draws over them.
- `Draw(display)`: (re)acquire textures if in view, then rebuild the meshes: Base = sheet rect clipped to the non-flap side for each fold (fan-triangulated, UV = local→[0,1]²); Flap = `LandedRegion` per fold, UV per vertex = `Back-space point beneath it` = `BackToFront(Reflect(v))` → `[0,1]²` (reflection is affine, so per-vertex UVs interpolate exactly); Creases = one thin quad along each committed/preview crease segment plus each remembered crease. Material property blocks set `_BaseMap` to the RT and `_BaseColor` to the state tint.
- Visibility gating (`LateUpdate`): the sheet is *in view* iff its bounds expanded by the largest possible overhang (`max(W, H)`) intersect the `ScreenCamera`'s world rect (the renderer finds the `ScreenCamera` once in `OnEnable` with `FindAnyObjectByType`; error and disabled if none — review N4). **Textures and cameras exist only while in view** (review N3): entering view creates both RTs (~6 MB each at default ppu) and enables the Front camera; the Back camera is enabled additionally iff any fold is displayed; leaving view disables the cameras and releases the textures. A retreat animation only ever plays on the current Screen, which is always in view, so the Back texture is never released mid-retreat; the Back camera stays enabled until the retreat ends. Sheets entering view mid-slide allocate their textures during the slide — a small hitch accepted for now (round-2 N7).
- Tunables (`[Header("Textures")]`): `pixelsPerUnit = 128` (`Min(8)`; 1408 × 1088 per face at default). (`[Header("Tints")]`): `previewTint = (1,1,1,0.85)`, `invalidTint = (1, 0.35, 0.35, 0.85)`, `retreatingTint = white`. (`[Header("Crease")]`): `creaseWidth = 0.06`, `creaseColor = (0.25, 0.2, 0.15, 0.9)`, `rememberedCreaseColor = (0.25, 0.2, 0.15, 0.35)`. Materials `faceMaterial`, `creaseMaterial` (asset references).
- Live: tunables are read on every `Draw`/`LateUpdate`; `pixelsPerUnit` changes re-create the textures on the next `Draw` (checked against the current size).

- State machine (`Update`): `Idle` → on `Grab` performed with a current Screen and no transition: local point `p`; if the Screen `IsFolded`: `TryUnfoldAt(p)` (crease within `creaseGrabDistance`), stay Idle. Else resolve anchor: nearest corner within `cornerGrabRadius` → corner; else nearest edge within `edgeGrabMargin` (and the point within the sheet's extent along that edge) → edge; else nothing. → `Dragging`: each frame `depth = snap(DepthForDragPoint(anchor, p))`, `SetPreview(new Fold(anchor, depth))`. On `Grab` released: `TryCommit(preview)`; either way `SetPreview(null)`. On `Cancel` performed, or a transition starting, or `OnDisable`: `SetPreview(null)` → Idle.
- Screen → world → sheet-local: `view.ScreenToWorldPoint` (ortho; z ignored), then `sheet.transform.InverseTransformPoint`. Never cached across frames (the grid slides).

Errors if no `IFoldRenderer` component is present — with render-texture compositing the main camera cannot see face content at all, so a sheet without a renderer is invisible; that must not be silent.

Same code path for Front (hidden parts removed) and Back (exposed parts kept).

### `WalkableOutline` (static, pure) — revision 2

The walkable area of a sheet with one fold is **`(sheet ∪ reflect(sheet)) ∩ H−`** — the base plus the landed flap (landed = `reflect(sheet ∩ H+) = reflect(sheet) ∩ H−`), where `reflect(sheet)` is again an axis-aligned rect (every legal crease is at 0°, 90° or ±45°). With no fold it is the sheet rect. `Segments(IReadOnlyList<Fold>)` returns the boundary as tagged segments:
- `∂(sheet ∪ reflect(sheet))` = the edges of each rect minus the other rect's interior (a segment minus an open rect is ≤ 2 segments), each clipped to `H−`, tagged `SheetEdge(direction)` — the direction is the edge's outward normal (for a reflected edge, the reflected normal — still axis-aligned).
- The crease line's intersection with `sheet ∪ reflect(sheet)` (two intervals merged), tagged `Crease`.
Sheet edges are exit candidates and the walls of last resort; the crease is always a wall (beyond it the paper is lifted — review B2 is satisfied by this instead of a lifted-region collider). This also makes the overhang walkable (Aaron, §10 Q6): the landed flap's own reflected edges are the boundary there. Multi-fold: the formula generalises to `sheet ∪ ⋃ reflectᵢ(sheet)` clipped by every `Hᵢ−`; only the two-rect boundary routine would need to become n-rect — noted in code.

### `SheetBoundary` (modified) — revision 2

- Subscribes to `SheetFolds.Changed`; `Rebuild()` on change and at `Start`: destroys/reuses child wall objects and creates, per outline segment, a solid `BoxCollider2D` wall of `wallThickness` just outside the segment (its own child so the crease wall can be rotated 45°), and per `SheetEdge` segment a `SheetEdge` child: trigger `BoxCollider2D` of depth `edgeDepth` just **inside** the segment, carrying the direction.
- Tunables: `wallThickness = 0.5` (existing), `edgeDepth = 0.5` (`Min(0.05)`, how far inside an edge the exit trigger reaches).
- With no fold this produces exactly the old four walls plus four edge strips.

### `SheetEdge` (new, runtime-only) — revision 2

`Direction`, `Sheet`; `OnTriggerStay2D`: if the collider is the player and `Dot(MoveInput, direction) ≥ MoveTowardThreshold` → `navigator.TravelThrough(this, player)`. This is the old `Exit` trigger logic, moved; nothing is authored.

### `ScreenNavigator` (modified) — revision 2

`TravelThrough(SheetEdge edge, PlayerMover traveller)`: as before, plus **room** (revision 3, pure): `entry = EntryPosition(...)`; player box = `Rect` of the player's `Collider2D.bounds.size` centred on `entry`; solids = the destination sheet's Front `TerrainRegion`s that are not passable by the player's `PlayerAbilities` (`SheetOcclusion.SolidFootprints(PlayerAbilities)` → world rects; the destination is always flat and Front is never posed, so face-local = world minus centre); `TravelRules.HasRoom(playerBox, solids)` (`Scripts/Screens/TravelRules.cs`, tested) is false if any solid overlaps the box → return without travelling (the wall behind the strip stops the player; nothing is logged — it is the rule, not an error). `ScreenNavigator` reads the collider and `PlayerAbilities` from the `PlayerMover`'s GameObject at `Awake`, erroring if either is missing (round-2 N6). The origin's own walls never take part (round-2 N1).

## 6. Behaviour

### Startup

1. `Desk.Awake` lays out sheets. `Sheet.Awake` validates, assigns layers, finds `SheetFolds`/`SheetOcclusion`/`IFoldRenderer`.
2. `SheetOcclusion.Start` → `Apply`: Back root identity pose; on the starting Screen Front occludees are told `Uncovered` (colliders on) and Back occludees `Uncovered` (off); on every other sheet everything is off (revision 3).
3. `RenderTextureFoldRenderer.OnEnable`: cameras, textures, meshes; first `Draw` with no folds → Base = full rect, no Flap, no creases. The sheet looks exactly as before (through one RT).
4. `FoldDragInput.Awake`: finds the `Fold` map and actions; errors if missing.

### A fold (edge)

1. Press with the cursor 0.2 units inside the south edge of the Screen → anchor `EdgeSouth`, `Dragging`.
2. Drag up 3 units → `depth = 1.5` (the edge follows the cursor: it now sits 3 units in). `SetPreview` computes validity: player footprint (world → sheet-local) vs flap ∪ landed. Valid → renderer draws Base clipped above `y = −H/2 + 1.5`, Flap = the strip reflected to `y ∈ [−H/2 + 1.5, −H/2 + 3]` textured from the Back texture (mirrored), a crease line; the strip `y < −H/2 + 1.5` is not drawn — Desk shows. The Back camera is a child of the Back root and renders Back-space whatever the pose, so a preview touches nothing but the renderer (round-2 N3).
3. Move the cursor over the player → invalid → Flap drawn with `invalidTint`. Release here → `TryCommit` returns `CoversPlayer`, preview cleared, sheet flat, nothing logged (expected interaction).
4. Release while valid → committed → `SheetOcclusion.Apply`: Back root posed (rotation 180° for a horizontal crease — a Back sprite appears upside down, as the underside of a real flap does); Back occludees on the flap turn their colliders on (clipped to the exposed part if they straddle the crease); Front `TerrainRegion`s inside `HiddenRect`: colliders off, or clipped to the visible remainder (≤ 4 rects) if partially inside; walls and exit strips are rebuilt along the new walkable outline (crease = wall; the landed flap's edges, overhang included, are exits if a neighbour has room). Renderer redraws with `Committed` tint.
5. Player walks onto the landed flap: nothing special — flat world; Back terrain there blocks/gates; walking off any edge of the walkable area toward a neighbour with room transitions to that neighbour's Front (Bible §6 `[LOCKED]` holds by construction — there is no Back exit object).

### A fold (corner)

Press within 0.6 of the NE corner → `CornerNorthEast`. Drag toward the centre: `d = (u + v)/2` so the corner follows the cursor. Crease at 45°. For `d ≤ 8.5` the landed triangle lies inside the sheet; larger `d` overhangs the west/south neighbours and the Desk — the Flap mesh is drawn at z +0.02 above their Base meshes at +0.05. `BackPose` rotation = `2·45° + 180° = 270°`: a Back sprite appears rotated a quarter turn, which is what a corner flap does.

### Unfold

1. Press while the Screen is folded: `TryUnfoldAt(p)` → distance to the crease segment ≤ 0.3 → unfold, **unless the player's footprint overlaps `FlapRegion ∪ LandedRegion`** — the full landed polygon, overhang included, not `HiddenRect` (round-2 B2) (Aaron, §10 Q5: unfolding with the player on the flap fails; `FoldRejection.PlayerOnFlap`, nothing happens). Elsewhere: nothing (no new fold can start while folded — `AlreadyFolded`; narrowest single-fold behaviour).
2. Model: fold removed, `Changed`, `Apply(empty)` → Back root identity, Back occludees `Uncovered` (colliders off), Front occludees `Uncovered` (colliders/exits restored, ability gating re-applied), walls and edges rebuilt for the flat sheet. Crease remembered.
3. Visual: `Retreating` fold with depth easing `d → 0` over `unfoldDuration` (0 = skip). The landed flap shrinks back toward the edge/corner. Then the remembered crease is drawn faintly until the player leaves.

### Leaving the sheet

`Sheet.PlayerLeft` → `SheetFolds.Reset()` → `Apply(empty)`, remembered creases cleared, `Draw`. Folds and creases do not persist (§8 `[LOCKED]`). `FoldDragInput` drops any drag when `IsTransitioning` becomes true.

### Failure modes (no silent failures)

- Layers missing → `FoldLayers` error with the fix; the main camera then draws face content directly (mask unchanged), so the game stays visible but folding renders wrongly — the error says so.
- No `IFoldRenderer` on a sheet → `Sheet` error; without it the sheet is invisible.
- `FoldDragInput` missing actions/camera/player → error, component disabled.
- `Fold` map or an action missing from the asset → `FindActionMap`/`FindAction` throw (`throwIfNotFound`), same as `PlayerControls`.
- Depth beyond `MaxDepth` → clamped in `DepthForDragPoint`; `TryCommit` additionally rejects `depth ≤ minDepth` (`TooShallow`) and any invalid fold.
- Render texture creation fails (unsupported size) → `RenderTexture.Create` returns false → error, renderer disabled.
- A `Collider2D` under Front/Back with no `IFoldOccludee` → error at `SheetOcclusion.Awake` (it would keep colliding under a flap).
- `edgeGrabMargin ≥ Desk.sheetGap` → warning at Awake (ambiguous presses between sheets).
- Occludee count is small; no pooling. Meshes are rebuilt with `Mesh.Clear` + reuse of the same `Mesh` objects, no per-frame allocation beyond the vertex lists when nothing changes (Draw is event-driven, not per frame; only the retreat animation redraws per frame).

## 7. Interfaces & seams

- **`IFoldRenderer`** separates what a fold does from how it is drawn (Bible §4). Replacing the renderer = a new component implementing `Draw`; `SheetFolds`, occlusion, input, geometry untouched.
- **`IFoldOccludee`** is the "occlusion notifies, objects respond" seam (Bible §9). `TerrainRegion` is its only implementer now (exits are not objects any more). "Floats up when covered" later = a component whose handler moves the object; blocks riding the flap = a handler on the block. Terrain's partial-coverage answer lives entirely inside `TerrainRegion`'s handler.
- **`FoldGeometry` / `ConvexPolygon`** are pure and hold every geometric rule (crease families, reflection, coverage, Back pose). Unity-free, tested.
- **Fold list**: `SheetFolds.Folds` is a list; renderer and coverage iterate it. The two single-fold pieces are `FoldDragInput` (refuses a second fold) and `SheetOcclusion.Apply` (poses one Back root), both marked.
- **Cutting**: folding cannot be cut (it is the game), but the renderer can be replaced, and input replaced, independently. Removing `FoldDragInput` from the Desk removes the ability to fold and nothing else breaks.

## 9. Assumptions (engineering)

1. Sheets, Front and Back roots are never rotated or non-uniformly scaled by authoring (already assumed by `TerrainRegion.WorldRect`); the Back root's runtime pose is set by `SheetOcclusion` only.
2. Occludee footprints are axis-aligned world rects (box colliders). Reflection across any legal crease (0°, 90°, ±45°) maps an axis-aligned box to an axis-aligned box, so `BackPose` never produces a skewed collider.
3. Face content is rendered *only* through the render textures; the main camera never sees the `SheetFront`/`SheetBack` layers. Content overhanging its own sheet by more than the Desk gap would bleed into a neighbour's texture — authoring rule, not enforced.
4. The Back camera is a child of the Back root so it always renders Back-space regardless of the root's pose; the Front camera is a child of Front for symmetry.
5. URP renders every enabled camera with a `targetTexture` before/independently of the main camera by `depth`; `Camera.Render()` is not used (unsupported in URP). Camera count is bounded by visibility gating.
6. Front partial-coverage geometry is exact: `HiddenRect` is a rect, so `footprint − HiddenRect` is at most four rects, each a path of the clipped `PolygonCollider2D`. Back partial coverage is one half-plane cut. No approximation (review B3).
7. `Exit` and `TerrainRegion` disable **colliders**, not components, because Unity sends physics messages to disabled MonoBehaviours.
8. Remembered creases are cleared with the folds on `PlayerLeft` — a crease persisting after the fold reset would be state that persists across screens, which §8 says folds do not; treating the crease as part of the fold is the narrowest reading of "persists visually after unfolding". Confirmed by Aaron (§10 Q3).
9. Overhanging landed flap is walkable (Aaron, §10 Q6); its reflected sheet edges are exit candidates like any other edge.
10. Room at arrival = a player-sized box at the entry position overlapping no solid collider the player cannot pass; triggers ignored. The player's `Collider2D.bounds.size` is the box size.
11. A new fold cannot start while a fold exists — single-fold exposure (Bible #1). The crease click is the only interaction on a folded sheet.
12. Preview folds affect rendering only; physics and occlusion change on commit. The Back root is always active; a Back object without an `IFoldOccludee` is visual-only (rendered into the Back texture, shown only where the flap samples it) and, if it has a collider, an authoring error reported at Awake.
13. Player footprint for validity = the player's `Collider2D.bounds` XY rect.
14. Only the Screen has live physics (round-2 B1). Engineering consequence of Aaron's Q6: an overhang must be walkable over a neighbour, and the player is never on any sheet but the Screen.
15. Leaving from an overhang's far edge arrives at the neighbour's entry strip like any other exit (existing `EntryPosition`), which is a visible jump backwards for a deep overhang. Narrowest (nothing new built); flagged to Aaron in the report as an open question (round-2 Q1): keep, or arrive in place when the current position is inside the neighbour with room.
